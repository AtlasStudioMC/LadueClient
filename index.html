About Ram Client

Ram Client is a free Eaglercraft client that runs Minecraft 26.3 and 26.2 right in your browser. There's nothing to download, so it works on school Chromebooks and any computer with Chrome or Edge. It comes with 20 built-in mods (FPS, keystrokes, zoom, CPS and more), free capes, a 3D skin wardrobe, instances and themes, and the Ram-Craft server is already in your Multiplayer list.

Looking for an Eaglercraft client with newer versions? Press Play to start Eaglercraft 26.3, or switch to 26.2 in Versions.
